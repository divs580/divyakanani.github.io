## 🚀 Featured Health Data Analytics Projects

### 🏥 1. [Emergency Department Operational Flow & Capacity Optimization](https://github.com/divs580/er-patient-flow-analytics)
* **Tech Stack:** SQL, Python, Tableau
* **Project Overview:** Built an end-to-end operational analytics pipeline analyzing throughput, triage flow, and revenue across **10,000 ED visits** ($44.68M billed gross revenue).
* **Key Achievements & Insights:**
  * Designed a relational database schema with domain integrity constraints (`CHECK` rules) and queried operational metrics using Common Table Expressions (CTEs) and window functions (`AVG() OVER`, `DENSE_RANK`).
  * Modeled physician delays in Python and Tableau ($r = -0.73$), demonstrating that wait times crossing the **120-minute critical threshold** caused patient satisfaction to drop sharply below **3.0/10**.
  * Isolated walkout drivers (**5.41% AMA rate; 541 cases**), uncovering that **16.27% of walkout encounters endured waits > 120 minutes** before leaving.
  * Engineered a 5-panel Tableau executive dashboard mapping peak arrival surges (**14:00–20:00; >600 arrivals/hr**) to recommend an NP/PA-led Fast-Track unit for low-acuity cases and an automated 90-minute EHR wait-time notification alert.
* **Deliverables:** [Interactive Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/divya.kanani/viz/Ed_dashboard/Dashboard1)

### 👵 2. [Identifying Key Healthcare Assistance Needs for Older Adults in Maryland](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/maryland-elderly-healthcare-needs)
* **Tech Stack:** Python, Unsupervised Machine Learning (K-Means)
* **Project Overview:** Analyzed clinical, demographic, and financial disparities across **53,000+ patient records (73.6% aged 65+)** to guide state public health budget allocation.
* **Key Achievements & Insights:**
  * Discovered that insurance covered only **1%–4%** of the **$1.27M average elderly healthcare expense**, with the largest coverage deficit concentrated among adults aged **90–94**.
  * Applied **K-Means clustering** to segment older adults into **4 distinct risk tiers**, isolating high-expense cohorts in need of subsidized prescription assistance programs.
  * Mapped county-level geographic disparities across **Charles, Queen Anne's, and Howard counties** to recommend targeted mobile health clinics and community support networks.
* **Deliverables:** [Case Study & Policy Brief](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/maryland-elderly-healthcare-needs#readme) • [Jupyter Analysis Notebooks](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/maryland-elderly-healthcare-needs/tree/main/notebooks)
