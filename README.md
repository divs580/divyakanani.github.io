## 🚀 Featured Healthcare Analytics Projects

### 🏥 1. [Emergency Department Operational Flow & Capacity Optimization](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/er-patient-flow-analytics)
* **Tech Stack:** SQL (MySQL/PostgreSQL), Python (Pandas, Seaborn), Tableau Public
* **Overview:** Evaluated clinical throughput, triage stratification, and revenue realization across **10,000 emergency patient encounters** ($44.68M billed gross revenue) to identify operational bottlenecks and walkout drivers.
* **Key Achievements & Insights:**
  * Formulated relational schema DDL with domain CHECK constraints and benchmarked physician performance using SQL CTEs and window functions (AVG() OVER (PARTITION BY ...), DENSE_RANK()).
  * Derived clinical KPIs in Python (door-to-doctor avg 78.87 min, 21.17% admission rate) and modeled delay thresholds, proving physician wait times > 120 min caused patient satisfaction to collapse below 3.0/10 (r = -0.73).
  * Isolated walkout drivers (**5.41% AMA rate; 541 cases**), finding 16.27% of AMA encounters endured doctor delays exceeding 2 hours before leaving.
  * Engineered a 5-panel Tableau executive dashboard capturing arrival volume surges (peaking between 14:00 and 20:00 with >600 visits/hour).
  * Recommended an NP/PA-led Fast-Track unit for low-acuity presentations (Triage 4 & 5) and an automated 90-minute EHR queue notification system.
* **Deliverables:** [Interactive Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com) • [SQL Scripts](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/er-patient-flow-analytics/tree/main/sql) • [Case Study README](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/er-patient-flow-analytics#readme)

### 👵 2. [Identifying Key Healthcare Assistance Needs for Older Adults in Maryland](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/maryland-elderly-healthcare-needs)
* **Tech Stack:** Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn), Unsupervised Machine Learning (K-Means), Tableau
* **Overview:** Evaluated demographic, financial, and clinical patterns across **53,000+ patient records (73.6% aged 65+)** to identify structural coverage gaps and guide state public health budget allocation.
* **Key Achievements & Insights:**
  * Uncovered that while average healthcare expenses for elderly patients exceeded $1.27M, insurance coverage paid only 1%–4% of total costs on average, identifying an acute financial vulnerability among adults aged 90–94.
  * Applied unsupervised K-Means clustering to segment the older adult population into 4 risk tiers, isolating high-expense, low-coverage cohorts requiring subsidized prescription assistance programs.
  * Mapped county-level health disparities across Charles, Queen Anne’s, and Howard counties to recommend targeted mobile health clinics and community care subsidies.
* **Deliverables:** [Policy Brief](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/maryland-elderly-healthcare-needs#readme) • [Jupyter Notebooks](https://github.com/divyadhorajiya/maryland-elderly-healthcare-needs/tree/main/notebooks)
